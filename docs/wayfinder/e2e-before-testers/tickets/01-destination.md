## Question

Ben wants to "test everything and the e2e flow before I ask people to come
test it out and contribute". What does reaching that look like, and which
coordinators count?

## Resolution (Ben, 2026-09-04)

An outsider with a Sparrow laptop, a Bitcoin Core laptop, or a phone wallet
can generate a key on Corky, export the public key, receive, send, back up on
paper and as a Core-encrypted file, power-cycle, restore, and send again.
Every screen for that is decided, built, proven on the Zero 2 W, and written
down so a tester can repeat it.

**Phone wallets count.** BlueWallet, Green and Bull Bitcoin are researched
from primary sources first, because each reads its own QR dialect, then
proven with Ben's phone. This Mac has no camera; Ben's laptop has one and
runs Sparrow, so the Sparrow flow is QR in both directions.

Mainnet only, small amounts, as in the August proof.
